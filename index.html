Our story

A manor built on slow evenings & generous tables

Nestled in the rolling hills of Rosewood, The Majestic Manor began in 1987 as a tiny family kitchen inside a crumbling Georgian estate. Three generations later, the china is still mismatched, the bread is still kneaded at dawn, and every guest is still greeted at the door like family.

Chef Eleanor Ashford builds each menu around what the valley gives us — heirloom vegetables from Hollow Farm, lamb from the eastern pasture, and herbs cut from our own garden an hour before service.

“Come hungry, leave family.”
